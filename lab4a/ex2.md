# Chocolate Chip Cookie

## Ingredients

- 1 cup flower
- 1 2/3 cups butter
- 1/2 cup chocolate chips
- 1 table spoon vanilla extract
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 cup white sugar
- 2/3 cup brown sugar

## Cooking

1. Heat butter until melts
1. Pour **half** out into seperate bowl
1. Brown the remaining butter
1. Mix in butter with egg, white and brown sugar
1. Mix in all the butter
1. Mix in baking powder, soda and vanilla extract
1. **Beat it**
1. Mix in flower
1. **Beat it**
1. Use icecream scooper to place on baking tray
1. Add a bit of salt to top of cookie
1. Toss it in the oven at **350f**
1. Bake until sides brown
1. **Consume**
